Meet our team

Adenike Jimoh

Welcome to Oropo Visionary Coaching Co., where leadership takes center stage in character, conduct, and competence. Meet Dr. Adenike O. Jimoh an Associate Professor of Paediatrics at the College of Medicine and Allied Health Sciences, Bingham University, Jos campus, and the visionary founder and CEO of Oropo Visionary Coaching Co.

At Oropo, Dr. Adenike leads the charge in crafting a transformative coaching experience for educators. The company, dedicated to nurturing leaders in effective service delivery, places a special emphasis on educators, creative youths, and female leaders, recognizing these groups of people as the cornerstone of leadership development. As the lead coach for the TeachTomorrowTM leadership coaching course (TTLC), Dr. Adenike brings her wealth of expertise to empower educators through this transformative coaching course.

Beyond her impactful work with Oropo, Dr. Adenike is a distinguished Fellow in the Faculty of Paediatrics of the National Postgraduate Medical College of Nigeria and a Fellow of Population and Reproductive Health Initiative. Holding certificates in Project Management, Leadership and Management in Health, a master’s degree in International Affairs and Diplomacy (MIAD), and a Postgraduate Diploma in Education (PGDE), she seamlessly integrates her medical and educational expertise.

Driven by a passion for inclusive education, she advocates for flexible classrooms, envisioning a future where barriers dissolve, and every child has access to tailored learning. Dr. Adenike Jimoh is not just a medical professional; she is a dynamic leader, author, counsellor, speaker, and the driving force behind Oropo Visionary Coaching Co.

Lydia Oluwakemi Oyewo

Meet Lydia Oluwakemi Oyewo, a distinguished Notary Public and seasoned lawyer with over 15 years of expertise in various legal domains, specializing in commercial practice, litigation, and a keen focus on intellectual property protection, particularly in the digital space. As the Head of Practice at O.E.B. Offiong SAN and Co, Kano Office, she has provided invaluable advisory services to small businesses, medium enterprises, and multinational corporations.

Lydia serves as the Chairperson of the Christian Lawyers Fellowship (CLASFON) Kano chapter, reflecting her commitment to community engagement. She is an active member of esteemed professional bodies, including the Nigerian Bar Association (NBA), International Federation of Women Lawyers (FIDA), and African Women Lawyers Association (AWLA).

Beyond her legal prowess, Lydia is passionate about nurturing the next generation of female professionals, contributing to their development and capacity building. She dedicates time to read, finding solace and joy in quality moments spent with her immediate family.

Onah Ruth

Ruth Onah is a dedicated and result-oriented professional with a wealth of experience in administrative roles across various industries. With a strong foundation in customer service acquired during her time at Access Bank Headquarters and administrative expertise gained at Rutona Linkages, Ruth is adept at managing multiple tasks efficiently.

Akanni Ayobami Opeyemi

Meet Akanni Ayobami Opeyemi, a research-oriented Mass Communication professional and Digital and Communication Lead with expertise in strategic communication, digital communication, data analysis, and communication research. Currently pursuing his M.Sc. in Mass Communication at Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, where he also earned his B.Sc. in Mass Communication, he brings a blend of academic rigor and practical experience in teaching, administration, and academic support.

Ayobami has served as a Classroom Instructor at ECWA Academy, Ayeteju Amuro, where he designed and delivered curriculum-aligned lesson plans and managed classroom activities, and as an Office Assistant at Softech, where he managed documentation, office records, and computer systems. He is certified in Data Analysis by AltSchool Africa, Generative AI for Everyone and AI for Everyone by DeepLearning.AI, Strategic Planning and Execution by the University of Virginia, Intelligence Tools for the Digital Age, and Introduction to Cybersecurity Tools & Cyber Attacks by IBM.

Beyond his professional work, Ayobami has demonstrated leadership as President of the Charity Community Development Service (2022-2023), where he led weekly community development and outreach programmes, and as an Examination Invigilator at Ahmadu Bello University (2025). Proficient in Microsoft Office, Power BI, documentation, report writing, and communication research, he possesses strong organizational, analytical, and communication skills with a keen interest in digital trends, academic publishing, and leveraging technology for effective communication.
