Why partner with Oropo

We specialize in raising leaders who raise leaders, and we strive to strengthen individuals with confidence to lead and speak confidently in their own skin. Our core values of excellence, innovation, creativity, confidence and integrity drive everything we do. Our vision is to create a world where leaders are empowered to make a life-changing and long-lasting impact in their communities.

Ways to partner

Choose one, combine them, or tell us what you have in mind.

Sponsor an educator

Fund a place for a teacher on the TeachTomorrow leadership coaching course. We already offer scholarships to educators, and sponsors help us offer more.

Fund free leadership coaching

Back a cohort of selected leaders who receive coaching at no cost to them, as part of our corporate social responsibility work.

Bring coaching to your organisation

Commission a tailored programme for your staff or community. Tell us the skills you need and we will build the coaching around them.

Co-host a programme or event

Put your name beside a cohort, workshop or public speaking event and reach the educators and leaders who take part.

What our partners receive

  • Your organisation named on the programme you support
  • Impact updates from the cohort you back
  • Stories and feedback from participants
  • Opportunities to co-host events and sessions
  • A direct line to our founder and team
  • A partnership plan shaped around your goals
