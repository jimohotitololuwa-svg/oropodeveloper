WHO WE ARE

Oropo Visionary Coaching Co. is a highly respected Nigerian firm specializing in world-class coaching and educational training.

We specialize in raising leaders who raise leaders, and we strive to strengthen individuals with confidence to lead and speak confidently in their own skin. Our core values of excellence, innovation, creativity, confidence and integrity drive everything we do. Our vision is to create a world where leaders are empowered to make a life-changing and long-lasting impact in their communities.

Adenike Jimoh

 Welcome to Oropo Visionary Coaching Co., where leadership takes center stage in character, conduct, and competence. Meet Dr. Adenike O. Jimoh an Associate Professor of Paediatrics at the College of Medicine and Allied Health Sciences, Bingham University, Jos campus, and the visionary founder and CEO of Oropo Visionary Coaching Co.

At Oropo, Dr. Adenike leads the charge in crafting a transformative coaching experience for educators. The company, dedicated to nurturing leaders in effective service delivery, places a special emphasis on educators, creative youths, and female leaders, recognizing these groups of people as the cornerstone of leadership development. As the lead coach for the TeachTomorrowTM leadership coaching course (TTLC), Dr. Adenike brings her wealth of expertise to empower educators through this transformative coaching course.

Beyond her impactful work with Oropo, Dr. Adenike is a distinguished Fellow in the Faculty of Paediatrics of the National Postgraduate Medical College of Nigeria and a Fellow of Population and Reproductive Health Initiative. Holding certificates in Project Management, Leadership and Management in Health, a master’s degree in International Affairs and Diplomacy (MIAD), and a Postgraduate Diploma in Education (PGDE), she seamlessly integrates her medical and educational expertise.

Driven by a passion for inclusive education, she advocates for flexible classrooms, envisioning a future where barriers dissolve, and every child has access to tailored learning. Dr. Adenike Jimoh is not just a medical professional; she is a dynamic leader, author, counsellor, speaker, and the driving force behind Oropo Visionary Coaching Co.
